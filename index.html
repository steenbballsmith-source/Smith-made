    A few things to know

    Before we make it yours.

    Can I buy a custom sign instead of renting?

    Yes. Custom signs are built to order for you to own. Send your idea, approximate size, wording and needed-by date. We confirm the design, price, payment schedule and handoff in writing.

    How much does a custom wood sign cost?

    The quote depends on the size, materials, finish, lettering, supports and delivery. Share your budget or a reference image so we can discuss a suitable scope. There is no automatic price or confirmed production slot from submitting an inquiry.

    Can I collect my finished sign?

    Customer pickup by appointment has no transport fee. We confirm dimensions, vehicle fit and loading needs before collection. Delivery is quoted separately. Purchased signs do not need to be returned.

    Can I still ask about a rental?

    Yes. Rentals remain available by request, subject to the piece and date. Rental deposits, returns and pickup arrangements are separate from a custom purchase.
