Our Craft

Every piece starts in the shop, not a warehouse

Smith Made is a family woodworking shop in Greenville, South Carolina, run by Will and Steen Smith. We build carpenter‑scale wedding signage — the kind of pieces a calligraphy studio can’t make and a party‑rental warehouse doesn’t carry: seven‑foot arched walls, seating charts that stand on their own, a champagne wall, a rolling bar.

The biggest, most photographed pieces of your wedding shouldn’t have to be bought, shipped, or thrown away.

How It Works

From our shop to your Saturday

Weddings happen on Saturdays, and we only take what one shop can deliver well in a day. Tell us your date and we’ll work around it.

Step One

Pick your pieces

Browse the collection, then tell us your date, your venue, and what caught your eye. We’ll confirm the date is free and send a simple quote.

Step Two

Book it with 50%

Your deposit locks your date on our calendar and holds the pieces for your weekend — plus any lettering or engraved plaque you’ve asked for.

Step Three

We deliver

We bring everything to your venue on time and in one trip. From there it’s yours to place and dress exactly how you want it — your florals, your candles, your day.

Step Four

Last dance, we load out

We come back for the rentals after the reception, so there’s nothing to haul or store. Keepsake pieces go home with you, forever.

  • Simple, safe payment. Invoices come through Square or PayPal (goods & services). The balance is due at delivery — never before you’ve seen the piece.

      Questions

      Asked & answered

      How far do you deliver?

      Anywhere in the Upstate. Delivery and pickup is a flat $125 within 25 miles of Greenville — Travelers Rest, Greer, Simpsonville, Spartanburg, Anderson, Clemson, Pickens, Hendersonville, and Asheville are all in range. We extend the radius for mountain venues, and large builds never ship — we deliver them ourselves.

      How far ahead should we book?

      Weddings happen on Saturdays, and we only take what one shop can deliver well in a day. Spring and fall Saturdays go first — if you have your date, it’s worth checking now. The 50% deposit is what locks it.

      Can we personalize a rental piece?

      Yes, and we handle it case‑by‑case. Your names and date go on a separate engraved piece of wood that mounts to the sign — a plaque, a nameboard, a chalkboard insert — so the rental arrives personalized to you and the piece underneath is never cut into. We don’t engrave the sign itself unless you’re buying it; deep‑carved engraving is for pieces that go home with you. Tell us what you have in mind and we’ll say exactly what we can do for your date.

      What happens if something gets damaged?

      Rentals carry a refundable damage deposit — $100 or 25% of the rental price — returned after pickup inspection. Normal wear is our problem, not yours. We also carry general liability insurance and can send a certificate to your venue.

      Do you build things that aren’t for weddings?

      Happily. Anniversary and “established” signs, housewarming pieces, holiday commissions — winter is build season in the shop. Use the inquiry form and tell us what you’re imagining.
