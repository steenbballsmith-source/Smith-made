Before you get started

How long does a custom sign take?

Timing depends on the design, finish and current workload. Include your needed-by date; we confirm the production schedule in writing rather than promising a standard turnaround.

Can you make a sign for outdoor use?

Tell us the exact exposure, placement and intended duration. We must confirm the material, finish, supports and mounting for that use before accepting the build. An indoor design is not automatically suitable outdoors.

Can I rent instead?

Yes. Explore the event collection and ask about the piece and date. Rental availability and terms are quoted separately from custom purchases.
