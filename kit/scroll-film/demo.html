How to use this

Using the kit

1 · Copy this folder. Load scrollfilm.css and scrollfilm.js, paste the section above into any page.

2 · Point data-sf-video at a 10–20 s clip (landscape, one slow steady move). Encode it seek-friendly: ffmpeg -i in.mp4 -g 12 -an out.mp4. No clip yet? Delete the attribute — the poster image gets the same treatment, Ken Burns plus the particle dissolve.

3 · Rewrite the captions in the host site's own type and colors. The kit positions and times them; it never styles them.

4 · Reduced motion, no JS, no WebGL, data-saver: the section quietly becomes a poster with readable text. Nothing to configure.
